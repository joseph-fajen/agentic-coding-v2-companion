The Agentic Coding V2 course is organized into four phases that follow a deliberate arc: **Use → Understand → Evolve → Scale**.

## The arc

| Phase | Stage | What You Do |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **[Phase 1](/phases/phase-1/)** | Use | Run your first system — ship a real ticket through the full loop |
| **[Phase 2](/phases/phase-2/)** | Understand | Learn the five primitives the system is built from |
| **[Phase 3](/phases/phase-3/)** | Evolve | Make it compound — fix the system, not the code; then automate |
| **[Phase 4](/phases/phase-4/)** | Scale | Advanced/optional — teams, orchestration, AI-native codebases |

**Phases 1–3 are the core course.** A solo developer finishing Phase 3 has a complete, working system they understand and can evolve.

**Phase 4 is advanced and optional.** Independent deep dives for teams, complex orchestration, and production patterns — take what applies to your context.

## The through-line

The course has one central idea that runs through every phase:

> **Your coding agent is only as good as the system around it.**

- **Phase 1** installs that mental model and gets the system running on your work
- **Phase 2** teaches what the system is *made of* — the five primitives
- **Phase 3** teaches how to make the system *compound* — every deviation improves it
- **Phase 4** extends the system for teams, scale, and advanced patterns

## Phase 1: create your first system

**Weeks 1–3 · Videos V1–V11**

The "Use" stage. You install the mental models, stand up an AI Layer on a project you care about, and ship one real ticket end to end.

**Key concepts:** Layer 1 / Layer 2, the R-PIV loop, system evolution.

**What you walk away with:** A working system on your own project and the whole loop held in your head.

[Go to Phase 1 →](/phases/phase-1/)

## Phase 2: the primitives

**Weeks 4–5 · Videos V12–V23**

The "Understand" stage. You go under the hood and learn the five primitives every agentic system is built from: Rules, Subagents, MCP/Tools, Skills, Hooks.

**Key concepts:** Agent = Model + Harness, the composition order, "build the thing that builds the thing."

**What you walk away with:** Deep understanding of all five primitives and the ability to teach your agent to build them for you.

[Go to Phase 2 →](/phases/phase-2/)

## Phase 3: improve & scale

**Videos V24–V35**

The "Evolve" stage. You sharpen each piece, chain the pieces into workflows you can trust, and scale that trustworthy system wider.

**Key concepts:** Fix the system not the code, reliability earns autonomy, the human/agent division of labor.

**What you walk away with:** A system that compounds — every deviation improves it — and the ability to run it parallel, headless, and at scale.

[Go to Phase 3 →](/phases/phase-3/)

## Phase 4: going further

**Act-based, non-ending**

The "Scale" stage for specific contexts. Independent acts on teams, advanced orchestration, and AI-native codebases.

**Key concepts:** Shared scopes, team standards, durable execution, AI-native design.

**What you walk away with:** Deep expertise for your specific context — take what applies.

[Go to Phase 4 →](/phases/phase-4/)
