Hey everyone,

I built a **companion site** for the V2 course and wanted to share it:

**https://joseph-fajen.github.io/agentic-coding-v2-companion/**

## What it is

A reference guide organized for **lookup**, not sequential learning. When Cole introduces a concept in the videos, you can come here for the details.

The site covers:

- **The Five Primitives** — deep dives on Rules, Subagents, MCP/Tools, Skills, and Hooks
- **The Four Phases** — the course arc mapped out with what you learn at each stage
- **Steering Documents** — direction.md and engineering.md explained
- **Workflow Primitives** — headless, SDK, CI, and Archon
- **Coming from V1?** — what changed, what still applies, migration checklist

## Why I built it

I learn best by writing things down. As I went through the course, I kept notes on each primitive — what it is, how it works, when to use it. Turned those notes into this site so others could benefit too.

It's not a replacement for the videos — Cole's explanations and exercises are the real thing. This is the reference you come back to when you need to remember how skills work, or what the hook exit codes mean, or how steering documents fit in.

## Feedback welcome

If you spot errors, confusing explanations, or missing context, let me know. The source is on GitHub and I'm happy to improve it.
